# Music Industry Analytics

An analytics engineering portfolio project using Snowflake and dbt to explore artist growth, listener engagement, retention, and marketing effectiveness.

## Business Problem

A fictional music company has increased its marketing investment and experienced growth in total streams, but leadership wants to understand whether that growth represents stronger fan relationships or simply increased exposure.

The project will investigate which artists are acquiring new listeners, retaining those listeners, and generating deeper engagement to help inform future marketing investment decisions.

## Technology

- Snowflake
- dbt
- SQL
